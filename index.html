Macro photography

Focus stacking
for Macro

Bring the eye, the antenna and the tip of the wing into focus. MacroFocus combines the sharp regions of a sequence of photographs into one image.

View on the Mac App Store

Downloads open when Apple publishes the listing.

Apple silicon / macOS 26 or later

Inside the workflow

  1. Import a focus bracket

    Drag in a sequence shot at different focus distances. Preview each frame and choose which ones to include.

  2. Align and stack

    MacroFocus aligns the frames. Its default Depth Map method finds detail across focal planes and blends it into a single image.

  3. Inspect the detail

    At 100% zoom, slide the divider between a source and the result, or compare two renders side by side before exporting.

Technical
specifications

The native Mac app processes your photographs locally. Import a bracket, compare renders and export the version you want to keep.

System
Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later
Input formats
Sony ARW and DNG, or developed TIFF, JPEG and PNG images
Stacking methods
Depth Map by default. Weighted Average and Contrast Pyramid are experimental.
Processing
CPU alignment with Metal GPU acceleration available for stacking
Export formats
16-bit Adobe RGB TIFF, 32-bit floating-point DNG and sRGB JPEG
App Store purchases
Export free for 30 days from first launch. After the trial, choose an annual subscription or a lifetime unlock in the app. Stacking and previews remain available.
