Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

MacroFocus processes your photographs on your Mac. You can stack and export images without creating a MacroFocus account. The app does not upload your images or track your activity for advertising or analytics.

Photographs and project files

When you choose files or a folder, MacroFocus reads the selected images and their metadata to align the frames, build previews and combine the in-focus regions. File paths, fingerprints used to verify originals, settings and intermediate images stay in the local workspace.

Closing a normal project window clears its temporary workspace. If you explicitly open a saved workspace, it stays on disk. Original photographs and exports stay in the folders you choose. You can delete those files in Finder. Your own backup or cloud-sync settings also apply to those folders.

Mac App Store purchases

The App Store edition stores a first-use record in your Mac’s Keychain to determine when the 30-day export trial ends. This local record can persist after you remove the app.

When you buy or restore access, Apple processes the transaction. MacroFocus uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to read verified purchase status and determine whether export is unlocked. The developer does not receive your payment card details. You can manage your subscription in your Apple Account. Apple Privacy Policy

Licenses outside the Mac App Store

The direct edition and companion command-line tool use an offline license tied to a machine code derived on your Mac. Requesting a license in the direct edition opens an email draft with the machine code and the plan you selected. The developer receives those details and your email address if you send the message. They are used to issue and support the license. The app verifies the license locally.

Support and deletion requests

If you email Hengfei Yang, the developer of MacroFocus, your address, message and any attachments are delivered to the product’s Gmail inbox. The developer uses that correspondence to answer your request and, for registration, to issue and support your license. Messages and attachments remain in the mailbox until deleted.

To ask what information the developer holds about you, withdraw permission for further contact or request deletion of your correspondence, email hengfei.yang+macrofocus@gmail.com.

Visits to this website

This site uses no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Visiting these pages does not give the website access to your photographs or projects. GitHub Privacy Statement
